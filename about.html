About the Group

Research with a materials-to-application focus

The Adnan Younis Research Group is based in the Department of Physics at United Arab Emirates University.

Dr. Adnan Younis

Dr. Adnan Younis is an Associate Professor in the Department of Physics at United Arab Emirates University. His research focuses on functional materials fabrication, 2D materials, MXenes, heterostructures, electromagnetic interference shielding, energy technologies and environmental applications.

The group brings together materials science, physics, nanotechnology, electrochemistry and device-oriented research to develop functional materials with improved performance and practical relevance.
