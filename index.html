Research at a glance

Our group combines materials synthesis, nanotechnology, interfacial engineering and device-oriented characterization to address practical scientific and technological challenges.

01

2D Materials & MXenes

Surface engineering, heterostructures and functional integration of MXenes and related two-dimensional materials.

02

EMI Shielding

Lightweight, broadband and absorption-dominant materials for electromagnetic interference control.

03

Energy Materials

Materials for energy storage, electrochemical conversion and sustainable hydrogen production.
