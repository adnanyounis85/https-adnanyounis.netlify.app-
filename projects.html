Projects & Grants

From fundamental materials science to application

Our projects connect advanced materials research with electronics, energy, sustainability and industrial technology.

Advanced EMI Shielding Materials

Development of 2D heterostructures and composites for broadband, absorption-dominant electromagnetic interference shielding, with emphasis on lightweight and application-relevant materials.

Potential applications: telecommunications, electronic systems, sensing and other environments where electromagnetic compatibility is important.

MXene-Based Seawater Electrolysis

Interfacial engineered MXene-based heterostructures for efficient and corrosion-resistant alkaline seawater electrolysis toward sustainable hydrogen production.

Research focus: catalytic interfaces, durability, corrosion resistance and high-current-density operation.

Functional 2D Heterostructures

Design and synthesis of multifunctional 2D-based architectures with controlled interfaces, defects and surface chemistry for electronic, electromagnetic, catalytic and environmental applications.
