Research

Advanced functional materials for real-world challenges

Our research themes are interconnected through the design of materials, interfaces and architectures with targeted functionality.

01

2D Materials & MXenes

MXenes, graphene-related materials and other 2D systems; surface chemistry, structural evolution and functional integration.

02

EMI Shielding

2D heterostructures and composites designed for broadband, high-efficiency and absorption-dominant electromagnetic interference shielding.

03

Energy Storage & Conversion

Electrode and catalytic materials for electrochemical energy technologies and sustainable hydrogen production.

04

Seawater Electrolysis

Interfacial engineered materials for efficient and corrosion-resistant alkaline seawater electrolysis.

05

Environmental Materials

Photocatalytic and multifunctional nanomaterials for pollutant removal, environmental sensing and water treatment.

06

Functional Devices

Thin films, semiconductor nanostructures and emerging functional materials for electronic and optoelectronic applications.

Core capabilities

  • Nanomaterial synthesis and heterostructure fabrication
  • 2D material surface and interface engineering
  • Electromagnetic shielding characterization
  • Electrochemical and catalytic evaluation
  • Structural, optical and electronic characterization
  • Application-oriented materials optimization
